## Full description
Sonara is a simple, good-looking music player for the songs on your phone, with a built-in collection of free music from independent artists.

YOUR MUSIC
- Plays the audio files already on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- Search by song, artist or album.
- Create playlists and save your favorites with one tap.
- Pick up where you left off with Recently played.

FREE MUSIC TO DISCOVER
- Browse trending and new tracks from independent artists.
- Pick a mood (chill, focus, energy, happy, sad or sleep) and start listening.
- Every track shows its Creative Commons license and credits.

MADE FOR LISTENING
- A full-screen player with big, easy controls.
- Shuffle, repeat, queue and a sleep timer.
- Keeps playing when the screen is off, with controls in your notification and on the lock screen.
- Works with Bluetooth and headphone buttons.
- Light, dark and wallpaper-color themes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account and no ads.
- No tracking and no analytics.
- Your playlists and favorites stay on your phone, and you can delete them anytime in Settings.

Free music is provided by Jamendo. Each track is shared by its artist under a Creative Commons license.

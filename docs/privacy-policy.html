Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Sonara ("the app", "we") is a music player developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what the app does with information. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics or our own music servers. Optional online and location-based features send limited requests to the providers described below.

1. Information the app does not collect

Sonara does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. It does not access your advertising ID or photos and does not include ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools. Approximate location is requested only when you tap "Use location" for country-level music recommendations. Sonara does not request precise or background location.

2. Music on your phone

If you allow access to music and audio files, the app reads the title, artist, album, duration and artwork of the songs on your device so it can show and play them. This information is read on your device only. Your music files are never uploaded. Their details stay on your device unless you turn on the optional Lyrics feature described in section 4.

3. Information saved on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: your liked songs, playlists, recently played list, and settings such as theme. For online tracks, this includes the track title, artist, picture address and stream address so your lists keep working. This data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time in Settings > Delete all my data, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To let you continue where you left off, the app also saves your last queue and playing position on your device; you can turn this off in Settings > Downloads and storage.

4. Online music, regional recommendations and optional lyrics

The app can stream music from independent artists through Jamendo (jamendo.com). When you browse discovery sections, search Jamendo, or play an online track, your phone sends the relevant catalogue or streaming request directly to Jamendo. Requests include the information needed for the feature (such as a search word, mood or country code) and, as with any internet connection, your IP address and basic technical details. Jamendo handles this information under its own privacy policy and terms.

The Search screen can also search Apple/iTunes song-catalog metadata across the storefront for your device region and selected additional storefronts (Philippines, United States, United Kingdom and South Korea). The search term and storefront country codes are sent to Apple over HTTPS. Sonara displays catalog text and the official store link; it does not use Apple preview audio or extract audio from Apple Music. Tapping a result opens its Apple Music/iTunes page. Separate YouTube Music, Spotify and Apple Music search chips open the relevant official app or website; the search phrase is then handled by that service under its own privacy policy and terms.

Regional recommendations are optional. Sonara starts with the country in your device region settings (using the Philippines if no country is set). Only after you tap "Use location" does Android request approximate foreground location permission. Sonara uses the location result to determine a country and sends only that country code to Jamendo for regional charts and artist discovery. Sonara does not send latitude/longitude coordinates to Jamendo and does not save location coordinates. Android location and geocoding services used to resolve the country may process the location request under their own terms. You can deny or later revoke the permission in Android Settings; the app will continue using the device region.

If you turn on Lyrics in Settings > Player (it is off by default), the app looks up lyrics on LRCLIB (lrclib.net) when you open the lyrics view for a song. The request contains that song's title, artist, album and length, plus your IP address as with any internet connection. Nothing else about you or your music is sent. Lyrics are saved on your device so the same song is not requested twice, and you can clear them in Settings. LRCLIB handles requests under its own terms, available at lrclib.net. Lyrics are shown for personal reading only and are not stored or shared by us.

Songs you stream are kept in a play cache on your device, so playing them again uses no data. You can choose the size of the cache or clear it in Settings. If you choose Download for offline on a song whose artist allows downloads, your phone's download service gets the file directly from Jamendo and it is saved in the app's own music folder on your device. The play cache and downloaded songs stay on your device, are not shared with us, and are removed when you delete them in Settings or uninstall the app.

If you choose Share on a track, your phone's share menu opens. What happens next depends on the app you choose.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or storage on older Android versions): to find and play the songs on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen. This is optional.
  • Internet and network state: to stream music and show artwork.
  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): optional; requested only when you tap "Use location" to show country-level recommendations. Precise and background location are not requested.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep music playing when the screen is off or you leave the app.

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's Settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Sonara does not operate its own account or music servers. When you use Jamendo features, your phone communicates directly with Jamendo. When you search the worldwide catalog, your phone communicates directly with Apple/iTunes using the search term and storefront codes. When you open an external provider search, the selected provider receives the search phrase after you tap its chip. When you opt into regional recommendations, the country code derived from approximate location is included in the Jamendo request. When you enable Lyrics, your phone communicates directly with LRCLIB. These providers process requests under their own privacy policies.

7. Children

Sonara is a general audience music player and does not require users to submit account details. Optional features, including regional recommendations, should be used by a parent or guardian on a child's device when appropriate. Some music on Jamendo may contain mature themes; parents may want to supervise younger children.

8. Security

Data saved by the app stays inside the app's private storage on your device. Connections to Jamendo, Apple/iTunes and LRCLIB use HTTPS. External provider links also use HTTPS.

9. Your choices and rights

Sonara does not store your location coordinates or maintain an account about you. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings, and you can clear local app data as described in section 3. Requests already sent to Jamendo, Apple/iTunes, LRCLIB or an external music provider are handled under those providers' own policies. If you have questions about Sonara's privacy practices, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update the app's store listing and this screen.
